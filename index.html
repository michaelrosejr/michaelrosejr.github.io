Pam YouTube Reader

A private, single-user tool for reading its owner's own YouTube data.

What this app does

Pam YouTube Reader is a personal automation tool. It is used by exactly one person — its owner — to read their own YouTube account data through the YouTube Data API v3: the channel's playlists, the videos inside those playlists, subscriptions, and channel statistics.

The tool runs locally on the owner's own computer. Its purpose is to answer the owner's questions about their own saved videos (for example: listing playlists, finding which playlist a video belongs to, or summarizing a playlist's contents).

How it works

The owner authorizes the tool with their own Google Account using Google's standard OAuth 2.0 consent flow, requesting read-only access (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.readonly). Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Who can use it

No one else. There is no sign-up, no account creation, and no multi-user access. The tool is not offered as a service, is not distributed, and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Google or YouTube. It handles no data belonging to anyone other than its single owner.

Privacy

See the privacy policy for full details of what is accessed, how it is used and stored, and the commitments we follow under Google's API Services User Data Policy.
